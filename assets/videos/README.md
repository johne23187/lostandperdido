# Backseat videos

All five uploaded MOV clips are connected in `vanVideos` near the end of `script.js`.
Browser-ready MP4 copies preserve their original H.264 video and AAC audio without re-encoding. The original files are retained.

Videos start when entering the van and automatically loop through the five tapes, with half a second of grey TV static between clips. Use the arrows below the seats to switch manually, or the native controls for pause/fullscreen. If sound autoplay is blocked, playback falls back to muted; use the volume control to unmute. Portrait videos fit inside the TV without cropping. Closing the van pauses playback.
